Fall 2026

Mon/Wed, 11:00 AM – 12:15 PM · Classroom TBD · Prof. Casey J. Wichman, wichman@gatech.edu

Course details (PDF) — assignments, deadlines, and the full schedule. Syllabus (PDF) — course policies.
